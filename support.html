Support & Privacy Requests

For WoolAmp support, privacy questions, or a request concerning WoolAmp-controlled licensing records, contact woolamp@proton.me.

Privacy / licensing deletion

In WoolAmp, open Settings → Privacy & Network Services → Licensing & Privacy and copy the pseudonymous Licensing ID beginning with WA1-.

Email that Licensing ID to woolamp@proton.me. You do not need to send an Android ID, IMEI, MAC address, device serial number, raw Google Play purchase token, or private cryptographic material.

Removing WoolAmp-controlled entitlement data does not cancel or erase a Google Play purchase. A legitimate purchase can later be reverified through Restore Purchase. Minimal pseudonymous trial-eligibility information may be retained where necessary to enforce the one-trial policy, as described in the Privacy Policy.
